Precision CNC Wood Routing Services

Precision B2B CNC wood routing services for audio OEMs. Delivering ±0.1mm tolerance machining on Baltic birch, MDF, and solid timber speaker enclosures. Request a DFM quote.

High-quality contract manufacturing for speaker enclosures and premium audio components.
Woodsat offers premium CNC wood routing services for global audio OEMs. We specialize in high-precision contract cutting. Additionally, our factory uses advanced industrial machinery to ensure perfect edge cuts. Therefore, we can guarantee zero wood tear-outs and seamless acoustic joints.

Our CNC Wood Routing Capabilities

Multi-Axis Accuracy

First, our machines handle complex 3D profiles. For example, we create curved baffles easily. As a result, your acoustic design achieves peak performance.

Substrate Expertise

Second, we control cutting speeds precisely. This process protects dense tonewoods and composite board sheets. Consequently, the raw edges remain smooth and ready for final lacquer coating.

Acoustic Bracing

Finally, we route accurate internal pocket matrices. This step strengthens the speaker box materials. Because of this, our cabinet structures eliminate unwanted panel vibration.

The CNC Wood Routing Workflow

We use a multi-step manufacturing workflow to deliver consistent batch quality:

1. File Verification

First, our engineers check your 3D STEP files. We optimize the cutting toolpaths to reduce material waste.

2. Precision Cutting

Next, industrial CNC routers cut the panels. The machine drills driver holes and interlocking joints smoothly.

3. Quality Inspection

Finally, we measure every component part. This rigorous step ensures flawless drop-in fits for your drivers.
