FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

About Empty Wooden Speaker Boxes

1. What is an empty wooden speaker box?

An empty wooden speaker box is a speaker cabinet without drivers, electronics or internal components. It allows audio brands and DIY builders to install their own speaker components.

2. Can Woodsat manufacture custom speaker cabinet sizes?

Yes. Woodsat produces custom empty speaker cabinets according to customer drawings, samples, 3D files or technical specifications.

3. What materials can be used for speaker cabinets?

We provide MDF, plywood, veneer and solid wood options depending on structural requirements and appearance needs.

4. Does Woodsat support OEM production?

Yes. Woodsat supports OEM and ODM projects, including prototype development, small batch production and mass manufacturing.

5. Can you provide finishing services?

Yes. Available finishing options include wood veneer, painting, high gloss lacquer, polishing and protective coatings.

6. What information is needed for a quotation?

Please provide product drawings, dimensions, material requirements, surface finishing requirements and estimated order quantity.
