Our Multi-Stage Application Process

We use five strict procedural steps to guarantee professional execution for all order batches:
Surface Optimization: First, we machine-sand raw boards down to 220 grit. We apply grain fillers to closed-grain woods.
Sealer Application: Next, operators spray two dense layers of base polyurethane sealer. This step completely blocks moisture absorption.
Pigment Bonding: Then, we apply specialized wood stains or solid pigments inside pressurized booths.
Top Coat Spraying: Subsequently, we build up defense layers based on your selected aesthetic style.
Controlled Curing: Finally, we dry parts inside a climate-controlled room for 72 hours. This process ensures total chemical stability.
