An OEM speaker cabinet manufacturer produces custom speaker cabinets according to customer designs, specifications or samples.

Woodsat supports engineering, prototype development and mass production.

### Can Woodsat manufacture speaker cabinets based on drawings?

Yes.

Customers can provide 2D drawings, 3D CAD files, samples or product concepts.

Our engineers evaluate and prepare production solutions.

### Does Woodsat support small quantity OEM orders?

Yes.

We support prototype projects, small batch production and large volume manufacturing.

### What materials can be used for OEM speaker cabinets?

Available materials include MDF, plywood, natural wood veneer and solid wood according to product requirements.

### Can Woodsat handle finishing and assembly?

Yes.

Woodsat provides CNC machining, painting, veneer finishing, polishing, assembly and inspection services.

Send your product drawings, photos, samples or requirements.
