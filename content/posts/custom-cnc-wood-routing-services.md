## Our CNC Wood Routing Capabilities
{{< card-grid cols=3 >}}
{{< card title="Multi-Axis Accuracy" >}}

Second, we control cutting speeds precisely. This process protects dense [tonewoods](https://woodsat.com/best-wood-for-speaker-boxes/) and composite board sheets. Consequently, the raw edges remain smooth and ready for [final lacquer coating](https://woodsat.com/speaker-box-finishes/).

## The CNC Wood Routing Workflow
{{< band bg="warm" >}}
We use a multi-step manufacturing workflow to deliver consistent batch quality:
{{< /band >}}
{{< card-grid cols=1 >}}
{{< card title="1. File Verification" >}}
First, our engineers check your 3D STEP files. We optimize the cutting toolpaths to reduce material waste.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="2. Precision Cutting" >}}
Next, industrial [CNC routers](https://woodsat.com/custom-speaker-cabinet-builder/) cut the panels. The machine drills driver holes and interlocking joints smoothly.
{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="3. Quality Inspection" >}}
Finally, we measure every component part. This rigorous step ensures flawless drop-in fits for your drivers.
{{< /card >}}
